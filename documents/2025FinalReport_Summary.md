# Summary: 2025FinalReport.docx

## Full Title
Genetic and Demographic Studies to Guide Conservation Management of Razorback Sucker and Bonytail in Off-Channel Habitats and the Lower Colorado River Mainstem, Final Report (2025)

**Authors:** T.E. Dowling, B.R. Kesner, P.C. Marsh, and collaborators (Dowling lab, Wayne State University; Marsh & Associates; University of New Mexico)

**Submitted to:** Lower Colorado River Multi-Species Conservation Program (LCR MSCP), Bureau of Reclamation, Boulder City, Nevada

---

## Overview

This is the primary Dowling lab genetics and demographic monitoring final report covering both RASU and BONY across Lower Colorado River mainstem and off-channel habitat (OCH) sites through 2025. The report has four major components: (1) BONY GT-seq SNP panel development and monitoring; (2) RASU GT-seq monitoring across mainstem and OCH; (3) database development; and (4) RASU/BONY tissue archive at the Museum of Southwestern Biology (MSB).

---

## Key Sections and Findings

### BONY Genetics
- Developed a **238-locus GT-seq SNP panel** to replace microsatellite-based monitoring for BONY.
- Evaluated F1, F2, and F3 broodstocks at SNARRC; Ne ≈ 12 for F1s (similar to the 10 original founders); F2/F3 stocks maintain comparable diversity.
- At INWR (IPCA) BONY ponds (IP2, IP5, IP6) originally stocked 2017; IP5 and IP2 were restocked in 2022 and 2023 following die-offs.
- IP6 had larger Ne-LD and lower relatedness among stocked adults than IP2/IP5, suggesting better genetic starting conditions.
- In all INWR ponds, close-kin recruits declined over time — a positive indicator. Variance in reproductive success (VRS) was low across sexes and ponds.
- BONY pond sizes confirmed: IP2 = 12.5 ac, IP5 = 21.3 ac, IP6 = 8.8 ac.

### Close-Kin Mark-Recapture (CKMR)
- Parent-offspring pairs at IP2 and Cibola High Levee Pond (CHLP) used to evaluate CKMR method for abundance estimation.
- CKMR estimates showed some bias but fell within expected ranges. Further mathematical development needed.

### RASU Genetics
- GT-seq panel evaluated for mainstem RASU populations (Lake Mohave, Reach 3, Lake Mead). Ne stable in Lake Mohave and Reach 3; declining in Lake Mead since 2021 — a concern for long-term genetic health.
- SNARRC-derived stocking cohorts show low Ne and elevated inbreeding coefficient (Fis > 0), indicating derivation from few parents.
- **Yuma Cove Backwater (YCB):** GT-seq outperforms microsatellites for parentage. Many individuals contribute progeny annually; Ne and relatedness stabilized after initial decline. YCB described as a stable, self-sustaining population. CKMR provides reasonable census size estimates.
- **IP Pond 1 (RASU):** Early mortality reflected in lower Ne estimates for progeny. Parentage assignment less complete than YCB, likely due to uncharacterized holdover fish. New 2024 stocking not yet genotyped, explaining decline in assignment rates for 2025 samples.

### Key Metrics Emphasized
- **Ne (effective population size):** Large downward changes signal conservation concern.
- **Relatedness (r):** Values of r = 0.5 indicate parent-offspring or full-sibling pairs; management should minimize r.

### Genetic Database
- Online genetic database completed at http://lcrgenetics.net.
- As of August 10, 2025: 13,251 fin clip records (9,127 RASU, 4,120 BONY); 5,282 SNP genetic records; 1,538 larval RASU SNP records.

### Male Marker Development
- Y-linked male markers being added to the GT-seq panel to replace gel electrophoresis for genetic sex determination. Once validated, genotypic sex will be available for nearly every genotyped individual.

### Tissue Archive
- Over 20,000 tissue samples accessioned into the Museum of Southwestern Biology (MSB) Division of Fishes. Uses Whatman filter paper system for fin clips and vial-jar system for larvae.
- Digital records in the Arctos Collaborative Collections database; project data transferred to the genetics database.

- Primary source for GT-seq parentage assignment results used in `GeneticsAndSurvival.R` and `PopulationMonitoring.qmd`.
- Confirms pond-level BONY stocking history: IP2, IP5, IP6 originally stocked 2017; restockings in 2022 (IP5) and 2023 (IP2).
- Confirms RASU pond IP1 has uncharacterized holdover fish affecting parentage assignment rates.
